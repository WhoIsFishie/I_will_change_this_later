## Additional Info
Dr. Vishan has an academic background in tourism and business economics and completed his doctorate in the UK in 2013. He has also served as the manager of research and development for MATI.
His position required him to interact extensively with the IMF, World Bank, government ministries, private sector businesses, NGO’s and local communities giving him an insight into stakeholder issues in the Maldives at various levels. Currently Dr. Vishan is Managing Director of Champa Brothers Maldives[^1].
