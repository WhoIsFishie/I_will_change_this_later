## Honorary Consul
In 1986 he was accredited as the Honorary Consul of the Kingdom of Denmark in the Maldives and in 1989 as Honorary Consul for Sweden, Norway, Finland and Iceland. In the year 2014 he received royal order of the polar star from H.M King Carl XVI Gustaf King of Sweden.
He is currently serving as Chairman & Managing Director, Cyprea, Honorary Consul from Maldives to Kingdom of Denmark.
